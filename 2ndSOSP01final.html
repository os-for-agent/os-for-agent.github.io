AgenticOS 2026

The 2nd Workshop on Operating Systems Design for AI Agents

📍 Congress Hotel Prague, Czechia  |  📅 Sept 29, 2026  |  Co-located with SOSP 2026

The Challenge

The legacy OS is holding AI back.

AI agents plan, invoke tools, and collaborate continuously. Yet they are bottlenecked by decades-old OS abstractions—processes, threads, and sockets—that were never built for dynamic, semantically rich AI workloads. To unleash AI at scale, the OS itself must become agentic.

Core Themes

  • New Abstractions: Process, container, and multikernel enhancements for agent execution.
  • Dynamic Sandboxing: Lightweight runtimes for unverified, agent-generated code.
  • Semantic Scheduling: Resource management for multi-agent and LLM workloads.
  • Episodic Memory: Long-lived state for context and prompt histories.
  • Agent Observability: Provenance tracking and debugging for autonomous operations.
  • Self-Managing Systems: Agents for kernel tuning, anomaly detection, and recovery.
